02 / The commitment

One direction, picked.

From Round 01: Theme C — Pocket Coin, with Logo L·02 — The Coin. Below is the rationale. If you want to pivot to a different theme, that's a fork in the road — say so and Round 03 starts from there instead.

Theme C · Logo 02
The Pocket Coin

Why this one, in one paragraph.

Of the four directions, Pocket Coin is the only one that gives the brand a single ownable color (clay), a physical metaphor (the coin), and a warm-but-not-cute register. The Notebook is too cozy for a global app, the Calm Ledger is too inert, the Night Vault is too crypto-coded. Pocket Coin reads as both casual ledger and traveller's pouch — which is exactly where Maya, Siti, Carlos, and Aiko live.

The logo is a coin and a signature at the same time. Round it scans as currency; up close it reads as "pe" — your initials, on a tally.

Three reasons it earns the slot.

①
It survives the App Store grid.
Most finance icons are blue or green dollar-signs. Clay terracotta has zero competition — your icon will be the only one that doesn't look like a fintech in a 6-icon row.
②
It scales from sticker to splash.
The coin monogram is a closed shape — favicons, app icons, embossed swag, mono and color, light and dark, all look like the same brand. No multi-element logos to break at 16×16.
③
It earns multi-script.
"pe" is two letters that work in any writing system context — Latin, CJK, Thai, Arabic — without competing for the reader's attention. The coin frame carries the meaning; the glyph is the signature.
