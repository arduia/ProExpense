A quiet app
for noisy money.

Pro Expense is not a fintech app. It's a notebook you happen to keep on your phone.

Below: the DNA we pulled out of the PRD, where the brand should sit on the design map, four candidate theme directions, eight logo concepts, and a first cut at the visual system. Pick what resonates — we'll converge from here.

01 / Product DNA

What the product actually is.

Stripped from the PRD. Every brand decision needs to defend one of these four. If a logo feels banky, it's wrong. If a screen feels busy, it's wrong.

01 · Pillar

Effortless

02 · Pillar

Personal

03 · Pillar

Private

No cloud. No bank linking. Data stays in your pocket.

04 · Pillar

Worldly

Multi-currency from day one. Feels native everywhere on Earth.

02 / Personality Compass

Where it should live, where it shouldn't.

Most expense apps cluster top-right: cold, institutional, clever. We want bottom-left: warm, quiet, personal — but precise, not cute.

Feels like — a Moleskine, a Muji pen, an index card, the Things app, a market vendor's tally book. Tactile, hand-scale, finite.

Doesn't feel like — a bank dashboard, a crypto exchange, a robo-advisor, anything with a candle chart. No gradients-as-credibility. No "secure-looking" steel.

Voice — second-person, calm, declarative. "Logged. ¥1,200 for lunch." Not "Transaction posted successfully."

Avoid — gamification confetti, streak nudges, motivational copy, anything that celebrates spending money.

One emotional anchor — the feeling of closing a small notebook at the end of the day. Quiet, satisfying, done.
